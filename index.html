Destructive Action Protection

Checking protection status…

When enabled, this device asks for your PIN/password before restoring a backup or clearing all data. A recovery code is shown during setup. Turning off this protection also turns off the app lock because they share one credential. This offline safeguard helps prevent accidents; it is not encryption and can be bypassed by someone with direct access to browser storage.
